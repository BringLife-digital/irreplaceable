# How I made myself irreplaceable from AI
*By Aswin, founder of BringLife Digital.* ✏️ First draft: edit it to match your real story before publishing.

## 1 · I was scared too
When AI started writing code and designing pages, the work I was paid for looked like the first thing it would take. I stopped asking "will AI replace me?" and started asking "which parts of my work is AI taking?"
**Lesson:** AI replaces tasks. List yours.

## 2 · I gave AI the tasks
I handed AI the drafts, boilerplate, resizing and first versions. I kept the parts it can't own: choosing what to build, for whom, and why.
**Lesson:** the saved time is only worth something if you spend it on judgment.

## 3 · I learned to direct, not type
I learned to write the brief: the goal, audience, constraints, examples, what "done" looks like. Today an agent builds my sites, posts, and even a cursor-following kitten made from 8 AI videos, because I can explain exactly what I want and check that it's right.
**Lesson:** the clearest brief wins.

## 4 · I became the one who checks
AI is confidently wrong often enough. I test every page, read every claim and fact-check every number before it goes out. Clients pay for that trust, not for the typing.
**Lesson:** verification is the job now.

## 5 · I tied it to money
I stopped selling "AI" and started selling outcomes: a restaurant site that looks worth booking, a post that gets enquiries. I turned my own process into skills other people can use.
**Lesson:** if you can't say what it earned or saved, it doesn't count.
