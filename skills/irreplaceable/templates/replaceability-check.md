# Will AI replace me? The 10-question self-check
© 2026 BringLife Digital

These are tasks that AI tools already do well, so on their own they are losing value fast. Ask: **"Roughly how much of your working week goes on each? 0 = none, 1 = some, 2 = a lot."**

| # | Task | Why it's at risk |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Writing first drafts from a template or brief (emails, reports, posts, docs) | AI drafts in seconds |
| 2 | Summarising documents, meetings or research | Built into most tools now |
| 3 | Copying data between systems, data entry, formatting spreadsheets | Automation + agents |
| 4 | Answering repeat questions (customers, colleagues, FAQs) | Chat assistants and help-desk bots |
| 5 | Writing code from a clear spec, boilerplate, simple bug fixes | Coding agents |
| 6 | Translating or rewording content | Near-instant and good |
| 7 | Making slides, mockups or graphics from someone else's notes | Generators do a usable first version |
| 8 | Looking things up and compiling them into a list or table | Research agents |
| 9 | Following a fixed checklist where every decision is already made | Easiest work to automate |
| 10 | Scheduling, chasing and status updates | Assistants and workflow tools |

**Exposure score** = total ÷ 20 × 100.

Then ask the other side, **yes/no**: In my job I…
- A. decide *what* should be done, not just how?
- B. own a result someone measures (revenue, users, deadlines, quality)?
- C. have relationships people trust me for (clients, team, stakeholders)?
- D. am the one who catches mistakes and signs off?
- E. use AI to do the tasks above faster, and spend the saved time on A–D?

**Reading** (be honest, never alarmist):
| Exposure | A–E "yes" | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| 60–100 | 0–2 | Much of your week is tasks AI is absorbing. The job title may stay, but those tasks won't need you. Move time towards A–E now. |
| 60–100 | 3–5 | Your tasks are exposed but your role isn't: you own decisions. Let AI take the tasks and grow A–D. |
| 0–59 | 0–2 | Low exposure today, but you're not yet the person who directs the work. Build A, B and D. |
| 0–59 | 3–5 | Strong position. Your edge is using AI (E) better than peers; keep proving it with numbers. |

Name the 2–3 highest-scoring tasks from the first table: those are what AI takes first, and what they should learn to hand to AI.
