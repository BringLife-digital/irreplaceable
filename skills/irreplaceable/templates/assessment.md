Score each skill 1–5. 1 = never done it · 3 = do it sometimes, results vary · 5 = do it daily, could teach it.

| # | Skill | Question to answer honestly | Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prompting and context | Did your last 5 AI requests include goal, audience, constraints and format? | |
| 2 | AI-assisted data work | Could you get a correct insight from a messy spreadsheet with AI in 15 minutes? | |
| 3 | Critical thinking | When did you last catch an AI mistake before it shipped? | |
| 4 | Directing AI agents | Have you delegated a multi-step task to an agent and verified the result? | |
| 5 | Responsible use | Do you know what data must never go into an AI tool at your work? | |
| 6 | Business value | Can you name one number (hours, money, customers) AI improved for you this month? | |

Lowest two scores that matter for your role = your focus for the next 30 days.
