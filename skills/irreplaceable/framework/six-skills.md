# The 6 skills that make you hard to replace
© 2026 BringLife Digital · Inspired by Masai School's "6 AI skills that will make you irreplaceable by 2027" (June 2026), expanded with practices we use daily.

| # | Skill | You're good at it when… | Daily practice (15 min) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Prompting and context** | You give AI the goal, audience, constraints, examples and format, and get usable output first time. | Take one real task. Write a lazy prompt and a detailed one; compare. Keep a file of your best prompts. |
| 2 | **AI-assisted data work** | You can hand AI a spreadsheet and come back with a correct trend, chart and "so what". | Ask AI one question about real data you own (sales, traffic, expenses). Check one number by hand. |
| 3 | **Critical thinking** | You catch the confident-but-wrong answer before it ships. | For every AI output today, ask: what's assumed, what's missing, what would a sceptic say? Write down one catch. |
| 4 | **Directing AI agents** | You can delegate a multi-step job to an agent, give it guardrails, and verify the result. | Give an agent one real multi-step task (research, refactor, draft + review). Note where you had to step in. |
| 5 | **Responsible use** | You know what data must never go into an AI tool, and how bias, privacy and local AI rules affect your work. | Before pasting anything into AI, ask: is there personal, client or secret data here? Read one paragraph of your region's AI rules a week. |
| 6 | **Turning AI into business value** | You can say "this saved X hours / earned Y / cut Z" and show it. | Log one AI-assisted task: time before vs after, and what you did with the saved time. |
